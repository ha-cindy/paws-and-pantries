Why this exists

When money runs short, people skip meals so their pets can eat, or give their pets up. Cost is the top reason owners give when surrendering a pet to some shelters, and those pets join the ones already waiting. Help exists, but it's scattered and often out of date. Paws & Pantries puts it in one place, checked by phone or email, in English and Spanish.

How listings are checked

  • Every local place is called or emailed to confirm hours, who can use it, and that it's OK to be listed.
  • Each listing shows the date it was last checked. Listings are rechecked every month.
  • Places that can't be confirmed are left off, so you never drive to a closed door.

Privacy

No sign-up, and we never ask for your name or address. The site uses Google Analytics to count visits and taps (like Call or Directions) so we can see whether it's helping. Ad features are turned off. Your town search stays on your device.

How AI was used

I used AI as a helper. It wrote code with me, drafted text and Spanish translations, and searched public sources for leads. I checked every listing myself by phone or email, and every number here comes from the public data named on each page. A native speaker is reviewing the Spanish.
