Why I made this

When money runs short, people skip meals so their pets can eat, or they give their pets up. Cost is one of the top reasons owners surrender a pet, and those pets land in shelters that are already full. The frustrating part is that help exists. It's just scattered across county PDFs, state lists, and word of mouth. I wanted one place, in English and Spanish, that a person could open on their phone and actually use. Zuzu approved.

Where listings come from

  • Today, every place comes from an official public list, like the Middlesex County Food Assistance Directory or the NJ Department of Health. Each one shows its source and the date.
  • Next step, starting December 2026: I call or email every place to confirm hours, who can use it, and that it's OK to be listed. Confirmed places get a green "Confirmed by us" label.
  • Until a place is confirmed, please call before you go. Anything that turns out to be closed or wrong comes off the site.

Privacy

No sign-up, and I never ask for your name or address. The site uses Google Analytics to count visits and taps (like Call or Directions) so I can see whether it's helping. Ad features are turned off. Your town search stays on your device. If Ask Zuzu doesn't understand a question, I see the first few words (with numbers removed) so I can teach her new answers.

How AI was used

I used AI as a helper. It wrote code with me, drafted text and Spanish translations, and searched public sources for leads. Every listing comes from an official public source that I read and checked myself, and every number here comes from the public data named on each page. A native speaker is reviewing the Spanish.
