Small ways to help

Share a bag of pet food

FISH, Inc. in Piscataway lists pet food as a donation it accepts. Call first: 732-356-0081.

Source: Middlesex County Food Assistance Directory, Jan 2026.

Free rabies shots

Towns in Middlesex County hold free rabies clinics for dogs and cats, open to all New Jersey residents. Call 732-745-3100 for the next one.

Source: Middlesex County rabies clinics page.

Tell a neighbor

Know someone having a hard month? Send them this site, or tell them to dial 2-1-1. Help with pet food keeps pets at home.

Know a place we missed?

If a pantry, clinic, or rescue near you helps with pet food or low-cost care, tell me and I'll check it and add it.
