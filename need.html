How we made this

The need score averages four standardized measures: food insecurity (2023), poverty (2019 to 2023), the share of people with limited access to healthy food (2019), and unemployment (2024). The groups come from k-means clustering, checked against hierarchical clustering, which gave nearly the same groups.

Limits: county numbers hide big differences between towns, and no public data shows pet ownership by county.
