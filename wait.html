Which shelter pets wait the longest?

Every pet we keep at home is one less in a crowded shelter. Here's who waits longest once they're there, from about 75,000 adoptions at the Austin Animal Center (2013 to 2024).

Compared with a similar pet, holding everything else equal (age, sex, health, how they arrived, and year). Lines show the 95% confidence range.

Try it: how long might this pet wait?

What this does and doesn't tell us

  • It shows which traits go with longer waits. It can't predict one specific pet's wait: the model explains only about 13% (cats) to 21% (dogs) of the differences.
  • It's from one Texas shelter, so New Jersey shelters may differ. It only counts pets who were adopted.
  • The famous black cat bias is real here, but small next to age and breed.
